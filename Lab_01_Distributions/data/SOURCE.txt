discoveries.csv
  Source: R package 'datasets', object 'discoveries': numbers of "great" inventions and scientific discoveries per year, 1860-1959.
  Original source: The World Almanac and Book of Facts, 1975 Edition. Reference: McNeil, D. (1977) Interactive Data Analysis. Wiley.
  Columns: year, count. Written with write.csv(data.frame(year = time(discoveries), count = discoveries), row.names = FALSE).

rivers.csv
  Source: R package 'datasets', object 'rivers': lengths (miles) of 141 major North American rivers.
  Original source: World Almanac and Book of Facts, 1975, page 406. Reference: McNeil, D. R. (1977) Interactive Data Analysis. Wiley.
  Column: length_miles. Values unchanged; written with write.csv(data.frame(length_miles = rivers), row.names = FALSE).
