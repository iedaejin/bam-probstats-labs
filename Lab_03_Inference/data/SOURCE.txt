sleep.csv
  Source: R package 'datasets', object 'sleep': extra hours of sleep gained by 10 patients under two soporific drugs (group 1, 2).
  Reference: Student (1908) The probable error of a mean. Biometrika 6, 1-25. Columns: extra, group, ID. Values unchanged.

ToothGrowth.csv
  Source: R package 'datasets', object 'ToothGrowth': tooth length (len) of 60 guinea pigs given vitamin C as orange juice (OJ) or ascorbic acid (VC) at 0.5, 1 and 2 mg/day (dose).
  Reference: Crampton, E. W. (1947) The growth of the odontoblast of the incisor teeth as a criterion of vitamin C intake of the guinea pig. Journal of Nutrition 33, 491-504. Values unchanged.
